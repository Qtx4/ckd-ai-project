CKD Stage 1: eGFR >= 90 kidney damage mild
CKD Stage 2: 60-89 mild reduction kidney function
CKD Stage 3: moderate kidney disease anemia risk
CKD Stage 4: severe kidney failure dialysis preparation
CKD Stage 5: end stage renal disease dialysis required

Diet:
low sodium, low protein, avoid NSAIDs

Symptoms:
fatigue, swelling, high creatinine
